review cycle documented in this package. It is identifiable as the
starting point by:

- **35 references** (the revised version in `04_final_paper/` has 42 —
  seven were added in response to the review, all appended as `[b36]`
  through `[b42]` so the original 35 citation numbers are unchanged).

  two prior studies on this dataset (Buribayev et al., Airlangga) — the
  revised version adds a third, directly relevant prior study on the
  identical dataset (Hossain et al., `[b37]`), found specifically in
  response to the AI review's Major Concern M1.

  version adds Table I, a field-spanning synthesis, in response to M2.
- The **architecture section (Section VI-B)** is prose-plus-a-single-list
  only, without the stage-by-stage input/operation/output table added in

- **No reproducibility package, editable figure sources, AI-detection
  disclosure, or contribution statement** accompanied this version — all
  four gaps are addressed by folders `06`, `05`, `07`, and `08` of this
  submission package respectively.
